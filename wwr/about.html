History

Whitewater rafting has a rich history that dates back to the early 1900s. What started as a simple recreational activity has evolved into a thrilling adventure sport that attracts people from all walks of life.

Today, rafting is known for adventure, teamwork, and unforgettable outdoor experiences. It continues to bring families, friends, and thrill-seekers together on rivers around the world.
